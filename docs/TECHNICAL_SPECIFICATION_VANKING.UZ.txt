TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION

VANKING.UZ — Animated Mascot Music Show

1. Project Overview

Project Name: VANKING — Mole Music Show

Brand: VANKING.UZ

Video Duration: Exactly 15 seconds

Animation Style: High-quality stylized 3D animation with expressive cartoon characters, smooth movements, vibrant lighting, detailed particle effects, and a playful musical atmosphere.

Primary Objective:

Create a visually engaging, energetic, and humorous animated music performance featuring three mole mascots representing VANKING.UZ.

The animation takes place on a green meadow with three rounded hills under a star-filled evening sky.

Each mole has a unique outfit, musical role, personality, and choreography.

Colorful fireworks periodically illuminate the sky, and their falling sparks transform into the glowing white word "VANKING" before dissolving.

Critical Requirement: Perfect Seamless Loop

The entire 15-second animation must form a continuous, seamless loop.

The final frame must transition naturally into the first frame without any visible cut, jump, camera reset, lighting change, character teleportation, or interruption in movement.

The animation should look like one endless musical performance when played repeatedly.

4. Fireworks and VANKING Text Formation

The fireworks are one of the most important visual elements of the animation.

They must look colorful, dynamic, and magical while remaining visually consistent with the cartoon environment.

4.1. Firework Colors

Three main colors must be used:

- Blue.
- Red.
- Green.

Each firework must have its own recognizable color.

4.2. Launch Timing

Fireworks must launch at four-second intervals during the 15-second animation.

Launch times:

- 0 seconds.
- 4 seconds.
- 8 seconds.
- 12 seconds.

The launch at 0 seconds is part of the repeating sequence and must connect seamlessly to the previous loop.

The 12-second launch must complete its text formation and particle dissolution before the 15-second loop boundary.

4.3. Firework Animation Sequence

Stage 1 — Launch

Three glowing firework rockets rise into the sky from behind the hills.

Each rocket leaves a sparkling trail.

The rockets should rise smoothly with slight variations in their trajectories.

Stage 2 — Explosion

The rockets explode in the upper part of the sky.

Each explos


ion creates a large spherical burst of glowing particles.

The explosions must feature:

- Bright initial flashes.
- Expanding particle patterns.
- Colored glowing trails.
- Small secondary sparks.
- Natural particle dispersion.
- Brief colored illumination of the environment.

Stage 3 — Text Formation

After each explosion, a portion of the falling particles begins moving into a coordinated formation.

The blue, red, and green sparks gradually transition to bright white.

The particles assemble into the word:

VANKING

The letters must be formed entirely from glowing white particles.

The word must not appear as a flat graphic, static overlay, or instant text replacement.

The formation must visibly emerge from the firework sparks.

Stage 4 — Text Display

Once fully formed, the word "VANKING" remains clearly visible for approximately one second.

Text appearance:

- Pure white color.
- Soft glowing edges.
- Slight shimmering.
- Clear and readable letter shapes.
- Consistent letter spacing.
- Positioned in the upper central area of the sky.

Stage 5 — Particle Dissolution

After the one-second display:

1. The letters begin breaking apart.
2. Individual particles detach from the text.
3. Sparks fall downward under simulated gravity.
4. The particles gradually shrink.
5. Their brightness decreases.
6. The sparks evaporate and disappear before reaching the ground.

The dissolution must feel smooth, elegant, and natural.

Important Requirements:

- The text must always read exactly "VANKING".
- No missing, duplicated, distorted, or incorrect letters.
- Fireworks must not cover the characters' faces.
- The text must remain legible against the night sky.
- Each firework cycle may have subtle variations in spark distribution.
- The overall effect must be repeatable without creating a visible loop discontinuity.

8. Critical Seamless Loop Implementation

This is a mandatory technical requirement, not an optional visual enhancement.

The finished animation must repeat indefinitely without the viewer noticing where one playback cycle ends and the next begins.

8.1. Character Animation

All character movements must use closed animation cycles.

At the exact loop boundary:

- Body positions must match.
- Head rotations must match.
- Arm and leg positions must match.
- Microphone and saxophone positions must match.
- Clothing and accessory positions must match.
- Character movement velocity must remain continuous.

Simply placing characters in approximately similar poses is not sufficient.

Their motion must continue naturally across the boundary.

8.2. Environment

The following must remain continuous:

- Sky gradient.
- Star positions.
- Star brightness.
- Grass movement.
- Ambient lighting.
- Character shadows.
- Camera position.
- Camera orientation.

Any animated environmental effects must repeat without visible resets.

8.3. Firework Particles

No particle system may abruptly reset at the loop boundary.

Particles from the final firework must fully dissolve before the loop ends, unless their motion is deliberately continued through a matching initial particle state.

The first firework launch must integrate naturally with the final frame.

8.4. Camera

The camera must have identical position, rotation, and framing at the beginning and end of the loop.

Any camera movement must be periodic and continuous.

8.5. Audio

The soundtrack must:

- Maintain consistent rhythm.
- Have no audible transition.
- Avoid sudden volume changes.
- Use properly aligned musical phrases.
- Preserve continuity across the loop boundary.

8.6. Loop Validation

The final animation must be tested by repeating the 15-second video at least five consecutive times.

The transition between repetitions must be visually and audibly imperceptible.

Special attention must be paid to the transition from 14.99 seconds to 0.00 seconds.
